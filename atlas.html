The Brief

Build a credible cross-chain DEX experience from scratch

Opening

DeFi users have zero tolerance for ambiguity: bad slippage warnings, confusing confirmations, and unclear wallet flows can cost real money. Atlas DEX needed an end-to-end experience that felt trustworthy, legible, and fast.

The Challenge

The client had market research and a vision for a cross-chain aggregator, but no product design. The product needed to make complex cross-chain routing readable and reliable across Solana, Ethereum, and BSC.

Understanding the Users

Three audiences shaped the interface

Active DeFi Trader

Expert execution

Needs route transparency, price impact detail, and the best output value without unnecessary friction.

Liquidity Provider

Clear yield decisions

Needs legible APY, total value locked, position balance, and reward status without overload.

New DeFi User

Actionable clarity

Needs a low-friction path to swap and bridge with simple language and clear confirmation states.

Research, wireframes, and system-first execution

Key Design Decisions
01

Show routing path

Users see every chain and pool step in the swap route, not just input and output.

02

Contextual price impact

Warning banners inform without blocking expert action for high-impact trades.

03

Step-based progress

Transaction progress uses explicit step counts so users know cross-chain swaps are working.

04

Dual-wallet flow

Displayed source and destination wallets together for cross-chain swaps, removing hidden requirements.

05

Staking clarity

Minimal staking view surfaced APY, balance, and rewards only; removed technical noise.

Solutions & Tradeoffs

How each decision addressed the core challenge

Transparency over simplicity

Showing the full routing path adds visual complexity but builds trust. Expert traders need proof that the aggregation logic is working. New users can ignore the path and just read the result. This decision respects both audiences simultaneously.

Warn, don't block

High-impact trades carry real risk, but experienced traders sometimes choose them deliberately. Blocking the transaction would be paternalistic and would lose users to competitors. A prominent warning surfaces the risk without removing agency.

Progress over speed

Cross-chain transactions can take 30–90 seconds. Without explicit step feedback, users assume failure and retry—which creates duplicate transactions and lost funds. Clear progress state is the most important communication the interface can provide during confirmation.

Multi-chain context upfront

Showing source and destination wallets together forces the connection model to be explicit rather than discovered mid-transaction. This eliminates one of the highest-friction points in bridge UX: realizing mid-flow that a second wallet is required.

Data over features

Liquidity providers don't need more controls—they need more clarity on the metrics that drive decisions. APY, current balance, and pending rewards are the only numbers that matter. Everything else adds cognitive load without adding value.

What the Product Enables

Cross-chain swaps

Users can swap across Solana, Ethereum, and BSC with route transparency and trusted confirmations.

Bridge flows

Clear bridge paths and wallet context reduce the risk of chain mismatches and unknown errors.

Liquidity and staking

Providers can manage pools and stake ATS tokens with concise yield and reward information.

Reflection

What worked

Designing a system-first product enabled consistent routing, wallet connectivity, and risk communication across the entire DEX experience.

Constraints

The cross-chain nature required balancing expert detail with a simpler path for less experienced DeFi users.

Next iteration

I would validate the bridge flow with real users and evolve the dashboard to surface actionable portfolio insights.
